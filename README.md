# My Favorite Movie!!! 🎬

## *How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days*

My favorite movie is **How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days**. It is SO funny and I watched it like a million times. (maybe just 5 times.)

---

### What Is It About?

There is a girl named **Andie** who writes for a magazine. She has to make a guy break up with her in *10 days* for her story. But the guy, **Ben**, has a secret too! He made a bet that he can make a girl fall in love with him. So they are both tricking each other. 😂

### My Favorite Parts

1. When Andie gives Ben a tiny fluffy dog
2. The love fern (poor fern)
3. When they play cards with Ben's family

### Why I Like It

- It is funny
- It has a happy ending (I won't spoil it)
